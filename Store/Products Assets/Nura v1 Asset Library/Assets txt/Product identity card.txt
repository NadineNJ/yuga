# Product Philosophy

YŪGA Nura is inspired by the quiet beauty of Mediterranean interiors.

Rather than acting as a technical lighting fixture, Nura is conceived as a decorative object that naturally blends into calm living spaces.

Every detail celebrates simplicity, organic forms and artisanal craftsmanship.

The lamp should always feel timeless rather than trendy.

# Visual Identity

The lamp must remain immediately recognizable as a YŪGA product.

The silhouette should never change between images.

The density of the rope wrapping must remain consistent.

The lampshade proportions must remain identical across all visuals.

---

# Photography Direction

Natural daylight only.

Golden afternoon sunlight preferred.

Soft shadows.

Warm Mediterranean atmosphere.

No dramatic contrast.

No artificial studio lighting unless creating a white-background studio packshot.

---

# Lamp State

Default:

OFF

The lamp remains switched off in nearly all lifestyle photography.

Natural sunlight becomes the main source of illumination.

This reinforces the premium editorial feeling.

# Photography Rules

Always use the exact approved Nura design.

Maintain identical proportions across every image.

Natural imperfections are welcome.

Never redesign the product.

Never modify the rope pattern.

Never alter the silhouette.

Never exaggerate textures.

Maintain visual consistency throughout the YŪGA Origins collection.

# Creative Restrictions

• Black interiors

• Industrial style

• Neon lighting

• Heavy Bohemian styling
