# YŪGA Collection Page

## Section 06 — Lifestyle Collection Scene

### Objective

Present the YŪGA Origins collection inside a complete living environment that reflects the brand universe.

This section is not intended to sell a single product.

Its purpose is to demonstrate how multiple YŪGA pieces coexist naturally within the same interior and create a harmonious atmosphere.

The visitor should immediately understand the YŪGA aesthetic language:

* Natural materials
* Organic forms
* Soft light
* Artisan craftsmanship
* Timeless design

---

### Layout

Full Width Image

Desktop:

* Full width banner
* Large immersive visual

Mobile:

* Responsive crop preserving main products visibility

---

### Visual Direction

Warm Mediterranean-inspired interior.

Natural plaster walls.

Soft organic architecture.

Natural daylight entering from large windows.

Minimal decorative styling.

No excessive decoration.

Products remain the heroes of the scene.

### Styling Rules

Allowed:

* Wood
* Linen
* Cotton
* Jute
* Handmade ceramics
* Books
* Neutral decorative objects

Avoid:

* Bright colors
* Modern technology
* Metallic objects
* Plastic materials
* Excessive greenery
* Trendy decorative items

---

### Final Emotional Goal

The visitor should feel:

"These objects belong together."

Not:

"These are four separate products."

The section should communicate lifestyle, atmosphere and belonging before product features.
